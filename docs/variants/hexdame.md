# HexDame

Playable in ION’s Checkers branch, IL01A0. Source: [Christian Freeling’s rules](https://www.mindsports.nl/index.php/arena/hexdame/72-rules), including the movement, capture and Coup Turc pages. Checked 24 October 2026.

The radius-four board has 61 cells and 16 men per player. White starts. Men take three forward edge steps and capture in all six directions. Kings slide along the six edge lines and can land on any clear cell beyond the first enemy. Captures are compulsory; only complete sequences taking the global maximum number of pieces are legal. Men and kings each count once. Equal maximum choices remain available.

Captured pieces block until the full turn ends; no piece may be captured twice. Empty landing cells and the origin can be revisited. Promotion happens only when the complete turn ends on either far edge (nine cells). A man passing through the crown edge continues as a man.

Captured-out and blocked opponents lose. Threefold repetition is automatically adjudicated in ION, rather than requiring a claim; mutual agreement draws are supported. Chess’s material and fifty-move policies do not apply, nor does three-kings-versus-one automatically draw.

## Integration

`checkersRules` owns legality and state transitions. `checkersRuntime` owns a dedicated iterative alpha-beta computer with six search budgets. The existing native-engine adapter dispatches these variants to that backend; the six independent C++ chess programs are unchanged. `checkersStepView` previews only supplied complete legal route prefixes. Every committed move is revalidated, versioned and saved through the existing game endpoint. Library, sharing, online seats and Time gates use their existing owners.

Coordinates use the renderer’s upward axial r-axis. The initial opposing 4×4 diamonds are generated by q=b−a,r=a and their central reflections, for a,b in 1…4. ION’s file/rank notation is a presentation convention, not a claim to an official HexDame notation standard.
